Rules

  • Colour is never the only signal — every green or red value carries an arrow or a sign.
  • Amber-red marks a missed habit only, never a weight gain.
  • Cards: hairline border or light shadow, never both. 14 pt radius small, 18 pt hero.
  • Tap targets 44 pt minimum. Spacing on a 4 pt base: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32.
  • Figures are tabular everywhere so columns of numbers align.
